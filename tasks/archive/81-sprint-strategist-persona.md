### Architectural reasoning

- **Option B (separate persona) chosen** — the Project Planner owns task-file state maintenance (mechanical, deterministic work); the Sprint Strategist owns scope judgment (strategic, adversarial). Merging both would dilute the Planner's deterministic duties and soften the Strategist's pushback mandate. Separation preserves SRP at the persona level.
- **Gatekeeper authority is explicit** — "explicit authority to say NO" + "pushing back is your job" + "success metric is not how many tasks get done" directly implements the Manager's documented anti-overcommitment need and the Founder OS `<challenge_policy>` (honest disagreement encouraged).
- **Evidence-bound pushback** — the persona must cite the specific decision-framework question, operating principle, or bias each rejected task violates, preventing generic vetoes and keeping pushback grounded in the Founder OS rules rather than opinion.
- **MoSCoW + WIP limits** — forces an explicit scope contract per sprint, making overcommitment visible before it happens (Won't Do list) instead of discovered during execution.
