## Goal

Stop malformed `openui-lang` programs from reaching `openchamber_render`. Give the executor an explicit validity gate with exact component signatures, a plain-cell rule for `Table`, bracket-balance checking, and a named-argument ban. Mirror the rule into the HQ hands-protocol fragment, bump the system-prompt version, rebuild the shipped prompt through the assembler, and pin the contract with regression tests.

## Manager's Notes
