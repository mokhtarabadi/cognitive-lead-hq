# DEC-20260912-006 — Closure approvals with obvious voice-to-text typos (clouse, Ckosetask, Appriveed) are accepted as valid approval words.

- Category: process
- Session: 183
- Project: cognitive-lead-hq

## Verbatim (original)

> Approve for clouse

## Verbatim (English)

> Approved for closure (with a voice-to-text typo for 'closure').

## Rationale

Manager dictates via voice-to-text; intent is unambiguous despite typos, and asking for rephrasing wastes turns.
